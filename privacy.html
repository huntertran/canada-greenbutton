Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Your data stays on your device

All Green Button XML files you upload are parsed entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript. No file content is ever transmitted to any server operated by this application.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect a Google account, parsed data is saved exclusively to your own Google Drive appDataFolder — a private, app-specific storage space that only this application can access. No other person, application, or service can read or write to that folder.

We request only the drive.appdata, drive.readonly, and userinfo.profile OAuth scopes. We never read, write, or list any file outside the appDataFolder. You can revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions.

Weather data

When you enable the temperature overlay, the service address associated with your utility data is geocoded to coordinates using Nominatim (operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation). Only the derived latitude/longitude — not your name, account number, or full address — is then sent to NASA POWER or Open-Meteo to fetch historical daily temperatures.

Geocoded coordinates are cached in localStorage so subsequent loads do not re-contact Nominatim.

Local storage

Parsed billing data, rate profiles, app settings, and weather cache are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you have connected Google Drive. Clearing your browser data removes it permanently.

No analytics or tracking

This application does not use cookies, analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking scripts. The only external script loaded is the Google Identity Services library (accounts.google.com/gsi/client), which is required for optional Drive sign-in.

Third-party services

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) — OSM Privacy Policy
  • NASA POWER — operated by NASA; coordinates are submitted but no personal data is collected
  • Open-Meteo — Open-Meteo Terms & Privacy
